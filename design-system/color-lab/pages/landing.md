# Landing and authentication override

## Direction

Apple OS 26-inspired liquid glass over a full-bleed moving image. The glass is an optical interaction material, not a universal card treatment. The first viewport must explain reference-driven color transfer, local privacy, and the account action without scrolling.

## Typography

Use Geist 300–700 as explicitly required by the product brief. Display text is medium weight with compact tracking; interface and form labels prioritize legibility.

## Composition

- Desktop: brand narrative on the left and a persistent account glass chamber on the right.
- Tablet/mobile: narrative remains primary; authentication becomes a bottom glass sheet opened by either CTA.
- Video remains behind content with a strong directional scrim and a static color fallback.

## Authentication

- Explicit labels, username and password fields, visible login/register tabs, password visibility control, inline errors, submit loading state, and a remember-login control.
- Never claim cloud sync or password recovery while accounts remain device-local.
- Never store plaintext passwords.

## Liquid glass rules

- Use one strong specular edge, asymmetric internal light, backdrop blur and saturation, plus a soft offset shadow.
- Primary actions are white glass/ceramic surfaces; secondary actions remain transparent.
- Preserve 4.5:1 form copy contrast and visible keyboard focus.
- Respect reduced motion and keep video optional for constrained devices.
